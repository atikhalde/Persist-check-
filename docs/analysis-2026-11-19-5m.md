## Finding 1 — the 15-minute sell OR did not cause today's shorts

This is worth being exact about, because it is the natural explanation and it is wrong.

The opening-range gate is `mom = bar time − 09:15 >= OR_MINUTES`, and the first bar it lets
through is 09:30 for a 15-minute range. The IT short was committed on the **09:20 bar**
(`mom = 5`) — four candles before even the 15-minute range had closed, and therefore identically
under 30, 25 or 15 minutes. What committed it was `bear_core`: a Nifty 5m close under VWAP with
EMA9 < EMA21 < EMA50, 3/3 MTF bear and ADX 21 — none of which reads the opening range at all.
The pick then came from `dir_conf = 62`, which is above the 55 gate without any `or_dn` boost,
because the sell range was not ready to give one.

Today's session never exercised the OR change at all. It would only have mattered if the index
had closed back under the 09:15–09:30 low *after* 09:30, and it never did — it turned up.

## Finding 2 — the lock commits on one bar and releases on two

`DIR_CONFIRM = 1` and `DIR_EXIT = 2`: one setup bar commits a direction, two consecutive soft
bars release it. That asymmetry is the whole story of the morning. 09:20 committed BEAR on a
single bar at the day's low; the conf then fell to 53 and 54 at 09:35–09:40 — one and two points
under the 55 pick gate, so `dir_state` never reset, it just stopped picking — and from 09:45 it
climbed back over 55 while breadth was 12▲/3▼ and the index was green. The short side stayed
live for four more candles on a tape that had turned.

The pick gate is doing the releasing here, not the direction lock: the lock only lets go at
`dir_state`, and `dir_state` was never given two clean soft bars while conf was already below the
gate. 09:35 and 09:40 were exactly the two candles where AUTO's stocks were still catchable.

## Finding 3 — a short pick suppresses the long follow

At the 09:45 bar the engine's own numbers were: **PERSIST + AUTO 98, CDUR 94, PSUBK 88**, live
flow `AUTO▲ Q (88)`, and `PERSIST − IT 80`. The pick was IT short.

`_follow_fallback` returns `None` the moment a pick exists, and the follow path only runs when
`commit_side == 0` (or a weak bull). A committed **short** therefore blocks the long follow
outright — the strongest sector on the board was never scanned, and the block even said so out
loud on the same screen: `live flow against (AUTO)`.

The 10:00 candle is the proof of what it cost: the lock cleared, the *same* AUTO numbers produced
`SECTOR FOLLOW — AUTO leads (persist 99, day +2.60%, range pos 98, flow aligned)`, and the entry
that followed (BAJAJ-AUTO) was the day's best trade. The engine wanted AUTO from 09:35; the
direction lock is what stopped it, not the sector scoring.

## Finding 4 — the picker arrives after the fires have gone

`Signals not alerted: 39 fired before their sector was picked` — BHARATFORG 09:25, BOSCHLTD
09:20, EICHERMOT 09:20, M&M 09:30, MARUTI 09:20, MOTHERSON 09:20, HEROMOTOCO 09:35. Those are the

had their leftovers: UNIONBANK +0.5R, INDIANB +0.0R, HEROMOTOCO −0.2R.

CDUR is the same story with a worse ending: PERSIST + 94 at 09:45, 98–99 from 11:35, picked for
**30 candles** (11:45–14:45) and **not one entry** — every CDUR fire was already behind the pick.
A sector that sits at persist 98 for half a session and produces no entries is a timing problem,
not a scoring one.

## Finding 5 — "Score 10 Low" on a short is the buy-side score

Every short alert today printed `Score 10 Low`, which reads like a weak signal and is in fact the
*signature* of a short: `total` is the bullish strength score (trend above EMA20/50, close above
VWAP, RSI > 55, MACD hist > 0 …), and a short requires the opposite — `entry_sell` fires at
`total < 40`. A falling stock cannot score anything but Low.

That means the number on a SELL alert cannot be read against the number on a BUY alert, and no
score floor can be applied across sides. Today's three shorts at 10/10/15 and two longs at 57/72
are not a quality comparison; they are the same scale pointed in two directions.

## What was wrong in the messages (fixed today)

1. **Every alert with a skipped pivot was rejected by Telegram once.** `(< 0.5R)` is a literal
   `<` inside an HTML message: Telegram answered *"can't parse entities: Unsupported start tag"*
   and the alert was re-sent as plain text. Today that hit HCLTECH, MPHASIS, PSB and BAJAJ-AUTO.
   The line now reads `(under 0.5R)` and carries no angle bracket.
2. **"were never evaluated" was printed about a trade that hit T1 and T2.** The close-of-day line
   listed BAJAJ-AUTO as *"not checked this session (no 5m candle) — their stops, targets and EMA9
   exit were never evaluated"* on the same page as its T1 and T2 hits. A name that was checked
   all day and only lost its **last** candle now gets the smaller, true sentence, and the book is
   kept so the two cases cannot be confused again.
