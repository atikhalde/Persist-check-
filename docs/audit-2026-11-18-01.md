# 18 Nov — why the 5m scanner alerted no CDUR stock while CDUR was the persistent bear

Reported: *"why scanner did not pick cdur stocks even though it was persistent, start with 5 min alert"*.

Short answer: **the 5m scanner only ever judges a name on the candle its sector becomes
the pick, and every CDUR name had already fired its crossover 15–25 minutes earlier.**
A fire older than the decision candle is refused by the entry loop as `not fresh`,
booked as `fired before their sector was picked`, printed once in the 15:33 summary and
never revisited. Bot 3 has a labelled `⏰ LATE LEADER` alert for exactly this case
(`scan1m._catch_up`); bot 1 had nothing. So the sector could be the pick from 09:45
with `PERSIST − 93 → 99` and a qualified live flow, and bot 1 would still say nothing
about a single CDUR stock — which is what happened.

Fixed: bot 1 now sends the same labelled heads-up, from the same function, with its own
limits. Findings and the refusal table below.

## Evidence as given

5m persist blocks sent by the bot that morning:

| candle | CDUR | flow | conf | band | bear book |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 09:45 | −93 | 86 | 68 | BUILDING | MET 78, AUTO 66 |
| 10:20 | −99 | 92 | 83 | STRONG | MET 84, AUTO 67 |
| 10:25 | −99 | 94 | 86 | TREND-LOCK | MET 86, AUTO 66 |
| 10:35 | −98 | 95 | 96 | TREND-LOCK | MET 86, AUTO 66 |

Screenshots: `CDUR Short 92`, leader `CDUR SHORT ▼ | Concrete Dir (DIR)`, `PLAY BearIdx:
Long MEDIA / Short CDUR`, flow map `−11.7 STRONG BEARISH`, PERSIST − `CDUR 98 · MET 86 ·
AUTO 73`, Nifty −0.64% with breadth 3/12 TrendDn. Signal tracker: `FLOW CDUR▼ 09:20`,
`SECTOR CDUR▼ 09:30`, `SMART Ⓒ CDUR▼ 09:30`, `INDEX SHORT 09:35` — i.e. the CDUR short
was live from 09:20 on every other engine.

## Finding 1 — a fire older than the decision candle is refused, once, silently

`pipeline.run_once`, entry loop:

```python
if not fresh:
    if fires:
        hhmm, want = ...
        if any(s.time == hhmm for s in fires):     # opposite side on this candle
            _note_reject(st, "opposite", ...)
        before = [s.time for s in fires if s.side == want and s.time < hhmm]
        if before:
            _note_reject(st, "earlier", raw, f"{raw} {min(before)}")
    if early:
        _cancel_early(...)
    continue
```

`fresh` is `best.time == last.strftime("%H:%M")` — the signal must be calculated on the
**decision candle**. CDUR's stocks fired at 09:20–09:40; CDUR became the pick at 09:45.
Every one of those fires therefore took `before`-branch and `continue`, and the only
trace left was one line in the summary reject book, at 15:33:

```
fired before their sector was picked 6 · ABC 09:20 · ...
```

That line is an audit trail, not an alert: nothing in the 09:45 message, nothing at the
price, nothing actionable before the day was over.

Note the same loop is the *only* place a bot-1 entry can be born, so a name can only be
judged while its own sector is the pick. A sector that becomes the pick at 09:45 has, by
construction, already missed the crossovers of 09:15–09:40 — which are the crossovers
that made it look strong in the first place.

## Finding 2 — bot 3 has had this alert all along, bot 1 did not

`scan1m._catch_up` → `catchup_candidate(...)` → `format_catchup(...)` sends

```
⏰ 🔴 LATE LEADER SELL — fired before its sector was picked
CDUR · Sector CONSUMER ELECTRONICS | Market BEAR STRONG (100%)
Fired 09:20 as SHORT-BREAK @ 1180.40 · sector picked 09:45 · 25 min ago
Price now ... (x.xR from the fire) · SL ... (the fire candle's EMA9 stop, untouched since)
```

with gates: same session, inside `SCAN1M_LEADER_MAX_MINUTES` (30), the fire candle's
EMA9 stop untouched since, and the move from that fire no larger than
`SCAN1M_LEADER_MAX_R` (2.0R). It opens no position and takes no entry slot. Bot 1's
entry loop had no equivalent: the two loops disagreed about whether a pre-pick fire is
worth telling the user about, and the 5m bot — the one the user trades from — was the
silent one.

This is the same class of defect as `SCAN5M_REQUIRE_TARGET` (17 Nov, audit-04): a rule
that existed in one bot and not the other, invisible until a session needed it.

## Finding 3 — the silent refusal was counted nowhere the user reads

`_summary_rejects` prints `fired before their sector was picked N` only when `N > 0` and
only in the 15:33 summary. The 5m alert stream had no `not sent:` line at all, so a day
where six CDUR names were dropped and a day where six CDUR names were never candidates
look identical from the phone.

## What the day could and could not have alerted

Age is measured from the fire's *close* (`fire + 5m`) to the run's `now`, and the gate is
`SCAN5M_LEADER_MAX_MINUTES = 30`:

| run | fire | fire closes | age at that run | catch-up today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 09:45 (pick appears) | 09:20 | 09:25 | 26 min | **sent** — inside the window |
| 10:20 | 09:20 | 09:25 | 61 min | refused `older than the window` |
| 10:25 | 09:20 | 09:25 | 66 min | refused `older than the window` |
| 10:35 | 09:20 | 09:25 | 76 min | refused `older than the window` |

So the fix has exactly one chance per name and it is the candle the pick appears on — 09:45
for CDUR, the same candle the user's block shows the sector being picked. The 10:20+ blocks
could never have produced a CDUR entry even with the fix; they are the confirmation, not the
entry. That is also why the alert has to be labelled: it is the fire's own entry and stop,
taken 25 minutes late, and it is the user's decision whether that is still a trade.

Caveats, stated plainly:

* The 18 Nov run log and that day's `Scanner summary` block are not in this workspace, so
  the per-name refusal code for CDUR is taken from the code path above, not read out of the
  day's own summary. If the 09:45 run also refused CDUR names for a *different* reason
  (stop already taken on the way down, or no plan on the fire candle) the new summary line
  will say so — that is the point of Finding 3's fix.
* The CDUR stock frame itself (9 slots before 09:20, so the fire candle has an EMA9 stop)
  is a property of the feed, not of this repo; the alert is only possible when it exists.
  `no plan on the fire bar` is the refusal code when it does not.

## What changed

1. **`pipeline._consider_late` / `_send_late_leaders`** — the 5m entry loop no longer drops
   a pre-pick fire on the floor. Names refused as `earlier` are re-checked with bot 3's own
   `catchup_candidate` (one implementation, not a copy), and the surviving ones are sent
   **after** that candle's fresh entries, so the entry alert always arrives first.
   One message per name; a burst of two or more becomes one digest (bot 3's
   `SCAN1M_CATCHUP_DIGEST`), because 17 Nov showed one-message-per-name is how a rule turns
   into noise.
2. **Three knobs**, defaults = bot 3's, so the two loops agree:
   `SCAN5M_LEADER_CATCHUP=1`, `SCAN5M_LEADER_MAX_MINUTES=30`, `SCAN5M_LEADER_MAX_R=2.0`.
   In `knobs.AUDITED`, both workflows, README, and the `doctor` line
   (`late leaders on (<= 30 min, <= 2.0R)`).
3. **The summary prints it** — `_summary_catchup`:

   ```
   LATE LEADER catch-ups 2 (signal fired before its sector was picked) · not sent: stop taken since the fire 1 · already ran (chase) 3
   ```

   `catchup_skip` counts are re-used from bot 3's vocabulary
   (`stop`/`chased`/`old`/`bar`/`plan`/`none`), so the same word means the same thing in
   both summaries.
4. **bot 3's text no longer lies.** `format_catchup`/`format_catchup_digest` ended with
   *"bot 1 has none for this name either"* — true yesterday, false from today. The closing
   line is now per-bot: bot 3 says the 5m scan reports its own late leader, bot 1 says the
   crossover candle is over and no position was opened for it.

## What did not change

* **No position, no trail, no entry slot.** The catch-up is a heads-up on the fire's own
  levels (`⏰ … Not an entry alert`). It is not booked in `sent`, so if the name fires again
  on a later candle — this time with its sector already picked — the normal entry path still
  takes it, with a live plan and the EMA9 trail. Booking it as a trade would have put a
  stop that is 25 minutes old into the trail and reported hits that happened before the alert.
* **PERSIST is not a trigger and stays one.** The user's complaint is that persistence did
  not become an entry; the correct entry trigger in this repo is the stock's own 5m signal,
  and the fix is that the signal is no longer discarded. Nothing keys an entry off `−99`.
* **The 1m bot is untouched** apart from the two text lines above.

## Found clean while here

- `catchup_candidate`'s gates themselves: `_fire_bar` refuses a fire whose candle is not on
  the tape (`bar`), the stop check replays every bar after the fire (`stop`), and the chase
  gate measures from the fire's entry (`chased`) — the three ways this alert can be wrong
  are all closed before it is sent.
- The sector pick at 09:45 (`_summary_sectors`, one pick per candle, `· followed:` clause)
  is a separate mechanism and is not implicated: the user's own blocks show CDUR picked at
  09:45 and held to 10:35.
- `MAX_ENTRIES_PER_PICK` does not apply to the catch-up by design (no entry, no risk
  factor); the digest is the volume control, not the cap.
