Same day as `audit-2026-11-18-01.md` (bot 1 had no CDUR alert at all). This is the other
bot: what it projected, what it refused, and what its own messages got wrong.

| time | message | outcome |
|---|---|---|

Two projections, two cancellations, seven late leaders, **zero CDUR entries** — while
CDUR was the pick on all four 5m blocks (09:45, 10:20, 10:25, 10:35) with

## Finding 1 — the 1m bot cannot enter a sector that is already moving (by design)

`run_minute` projects only the **picked** sector's names, and a projection needs a signal
on the *forming* candle (`fresh = [s for s in fires if s.time == hhmm …]`). CDUR's names
fired their crossovers at 09:20–09:40, before CDUR was the pick at 09:45. By then their
signals were behind them: the bot recognised them exactly once, as `LATE LEADER`, and
`confirm_pass` refuses the same way (`no fresh signal on the closed candle`).

So the only two entries the engine was even willing to *project* after the pick appeared
were LGEINDIA (10:16) and BATAINDIA (10:52) — the two CDUR names that fired a **new**
window crossover while CDUR was the pick. Both were refused at their close.

This is the same structural fact as bot 1's, one level earlier: **this system enters on a
crossover, not on persistence.** A sector can be short every one of its stocks and be the
pick for 50 minutes and still produce at most one entry per name, at the moment its own
signal fires — and if that moment comes before the sector is picked, the entry does not
exist at all. `#39` made bot 1 *say* that; nothing yet makes it *trade* it (see
"What was not changed").

What the bot did do right here: it named the seven late leaders instead of hiding them,
with the fire time, the untouched EMA9 stop and the distance already travelled.

## Finding 2 — a cancellation never said which gate refused it (fixed)

```
❌ EARLY CANCELLED — SELL LGEINDIA (NORMAL SELL)
projected 10:16:06 @ 1714.80 · close 1712.10 (+0.16%)
did not hold · 10:15 close — no entry recorded
```

The close was **in the trade's favour** (+0.16% for a short) and the bot said "did not
hold". Every refusal but the per-candle cap printed those same three words, because
`confirm_pass` threw its reason away: `_settle(..., reason="did not hold", reasons=capped)`
— and `capped` only ever held the cap case. The `no_signal`, `opposite`, `no_stop`,
`no_target` and `oi` refusals were logged, never sent, never counted.

Fixed:

* `confirm_pass` fills a `reasons[raw]` code on every refusal (`no_signal`, `opposite`,
  `no_stop`, `no_target`, `oi`, `cap`), `_settle` maps the code to words and, when the
  close moved in our favour, says so:

  ```
  ❌ EARLY CANCELLED — SELL LGEINDIA (NORMAL SELL)
  projected 10:16:06 @ 1714.80 · close 1712.10 (+0.16%) — price moved in our favour, the signal did not repeat
  no signal on the closed candle · 10:15 close — no entry recorded
  ```

* the 1m summary counts them, in the same spirit as bot 1's reject book:

  ```
  Projected alerts 6 · of which confirmed at the close 2 · did not hold 4
  Not held: no signal on the closed candle 3 · no pivot target — the trade would have had no exit 1
  ```

That is the difference between "the bot did not take my trade" and "the bot refused it
because the pivot target was not there".

## Finding 3 — two messages for one pick window (fixed)

The ×6 digest at 09:46 and the single KALYANKJIL at 09:48 are the same window — the
catch-up runs on every minute of the forming candle, and a name whose frame or plan only
became available a minute later is caught on a later tick. The second message opened with
the same `fired before its sector was picked` and the same `sector picked 09:45`, so it
reads as a duplicate of the first.

Fixed: a follow-up of a window that has already reported says so —
`⏰ 🔴 LATE LEADER SELL — 2 more from the same pick` — and the count comes from the
catch-up book (`{raw: {bar: …}}`), not from the tick.

## Finding 4 — the catch-up quoted two different risks (fixed)

```
Risk if taken now ≈ 10.42 (1.55%)  |  next pivot target: T1 S1 667.67 (3.1R, -1.61%)
```

10.42 is the risk from the price now (671.75 → 682.17); `3.1R` is the target's distance
from the **fire** entry (678.60 → 667.67 ÷ 3.57). Both numbers were right, about different
trades, on one line. From the price on screen that target is **0.4R**.

Fixed: the catch-up renders its targets from the price now (`_targets_now`, a copy of the
plan with `entry`/`risk` replaced), so the R on the target and the risk next to it describe
the same trade:

```
Risk if taken now ≈ 10.42 (1.55%)  |  next pivot target: T1 S1 667.67 (0.4R, -0.61%) · T2 S2 652.58 (1.8R, -2.85% ⚠) · T3 S3 628.97 (4.1R, -6.37% ⚠)
```

The `+1.9R from the fire` on the line above is unchanged and labelled: it is the move that
has already happened, which is the number that tells you whether the catch-up is a chase.

## Finding 5 — "bot 1 has none for these either" (already fixed in #39, verified)

Both the digest and the single message ended with *"no 5m signal fired on this candle; bot 1
has none for these either"*. True on 18 Nov, false since #39 — bot 1 now sends its own
labelled late leader. The closing line is per-bot, and the digest says "the 5m scan reports
its own ⏰ LATE LEADER".

Note the consequence, deliberately accepted: on a morning like this one both bots now
report the same seven names (bot 3 up to four minutes earlier, bot 1 at the candle's close).
Two labelled heads-ups beat one silent bot; `SCAN5M_LEADER_CATCHUP=0` silences bot 1's if
the duplication is worse than the risk of missing it.

## Found clean

* **`Score 10 Low` / `Score 15 Low` are not defects.** The 0–100 score is a *bullish*
  strength score; a short needs `total < 40` (`entry_sell = agg_sell and total < 40`), so
  "Low" is the qualifying side for a SELL. A high score on a SELL would be the bug.
* Projection gates match the close pass: pivot target (`require_target`), OI
  (`_oi_mode() == "require"`), EMA9 stop, per-candle cap, sector lead, `at_close` handover.
  No parity gap of the `SCAN5M_REQUIRE_TARGET` kind left in this path.
* `_early_allowed`'s retry budget: a cancelled projection may be retried on a *later*
  candle of the same session (`SCAN1M_EARLY_RETRIES`), which is why LGEINDIA at 10:16 did
  not silence the name for the day.
* The `EARLY CANCELLED` price delta is signed so that "beyond" always means "in our
  favour" for both sides; the 18 Nov numbers (-0.14% BATAINDIA, +0.16% LGEINDIA) are
  consistent with the closes.

## What was not changed — and why

**A late entry.** The one thing that would have turned 18 Nov's persistence into money is
entering a name whose crossover fired before the pick — the catch-up already computes
everything needed (fire time, age, untouched stop, distance travelled: KALYANKJIL +1.9R,
HAVELLS +1.3R). It is still not an entry, because it is a **trading rule change** and the
repo's rule is that those ship behind a knob with a default chosen by measurement:

* 18 Nov's seven catch-ups were all in profit 25 minutes after the pick, but they were also
  the reason bot 3's catch-up was built as an alert: on 17 Nov, 17 catch-ups in 35 minutes
  had a **median move of +0.2R from the fire** — entering them is a chase, not an entry.
* A late entry would take a stop that is 25 minutes old as its risk, at a worse price, with
  the target already partly consumed. Measured against the 17 Nov sample it is a losing
  rule; measured only against 18 Nov it looks like a winner. One day is not a measurement.

Where it stands: `SCAN5M_LEADER_CATCHUP=1` tells you about it, with the fire's own
entry/stop/targets on screen, so the decision is yours in the moment. If you want it taken
automatically, the change is small (`catchup_candidate` → `new_position` with `entry=price
now`) but its default has to come from

```
python -m nse_scanner backtest --date 2026-11-12   # … through 2026-11-17
```

run with the rule on and off (there is no flag for it yet: it would be
`SCAN5M_LATE_ENTRY=0` in the environment, the same shape as every other audited knob). That
needs the day files for those sessions, not another live alert. Say the word and I will
build it behind that knob, default off, so the replay decides whether it ever goes on.
