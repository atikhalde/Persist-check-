# Audit — every alert in the system, from the beginning

Not a session audit this time: an audit of **the alert set itself**. Every
message both bots can send, what makes each one silent, and whether that silence
is a decision or a hole. Four holes were found; all four are fixed here.

The test is the one the user keeps applying to the picks — *if the scanner did
something, can I read about it?* A refused entry, a dropped trade, a candle that
never arrived: silence is only acceptable when it means "there was nothing to
say", never when it means "there was something and nobody wrote it down".

## 1. The inventory

Bot 1 (5m, `pipeline`) and bot 3 (1m, `scan1m`). "Same function" means the code
literally is shared, not copied.

| # | alert | trigger | silent when | verdict |
|---|-------|---------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | `NSE live scanner started` | first cycle of a session | restarts, handovers (flag is stored) | by design |
| 2 | `MARKET / SECTOR` block | direction label, pick+side, or conf moves `MARKET_CONF_DELTA` | the scan is later than `MAX_ALERT_LAG_SECONDS` after the close | **deferred, not lost** — state is only advanced after a successful send, so the next cycle prints it |
| 3 | `⚡ EARLY … (unconfirmed)` | a stock fires on the still-forming candle | `EARLY_ALERT_SECONDS=0`, no 1m data, or the price-now bar is stale | by design (the close still alerts) |
| 4 | `❌ EARLY CANCELLED` / `⚠ EARLY UNVERIFIED` | a heads-up that does not survive the close | bot 3 only, when `SCAN1M_CANCEL_MESSAGE=0` | **now on by default** (see 5.1) |
| 5 | entry | a name fires on the closed candle, clears ranking + `MAX_ENTRIES_PER_PICK` | see the reject table below — every one is counted | ✓ audible |
| 6 | `✅ CONFIRMED` header | the entry follows an EARLY heads-up for the same name | — | ✓ |
| 7 | `🎯 TARGET HIT` | any of T1–T3 first touched on a closed candle | never — one line per candle however many levels | ✓ |
| 8 | `🛑 SL HIT` | stop touched intrabar (evaluated on the closed candle) | never; says when the stop was at breakeven | ✓ |
| 9 | `🔔 EMA9 EXIT` | close beyond EMA9 by the exit band | never | ✓ |
| 10 | `⏰ LATE LEADER` (bot 3) | a sector's leader fired before its sector was picked | `SCAN1M_LEADER_CATCHUP=0`, or the trade is already invalid | ✓ refusal reasons are printed |
| 11 | `⚠ Yahoo feed is delayed` | the 1m feed is >2 min behind the close | once per session only | ✓ |
| 12 | session summary | 15:33, once | nothing to report (no candles) | ✓ |
| 13 | `⚠ TRADE ABANDONED` | a position from an earlier session is dropped | — | **new** (see 2.2) |

## 2. The holes

### 2.1 A trade still open at the close had no message at all

Every other summary line counts what was *sent*. A position opened at 15:20 has
had no TARGET HIT, no SL HIT and no EMA9 EXIT, and nothing in the day's forty
messages says so — the summary said `Entries 10` and stopped. The user cannot
tell which of the ten are finished.

`fixed` — `_summary_open`: `Open at the close — no exit alert yet: LODHA
1208.20 (stop 1208.20) · BANKINDIA 155.12 (stop 155.12). A trade is intraday: it
is not trailed into the next session.` When the book is flat it says
`Open at the close: none — every entry got its exit alert`, so a flat day is a
statement rather than an absence. Bot 3 prints the same line (shared function).

### 2.2 A trade left open overnight vanished

`_track_positions` dropped any position from an earlier session with a
`log.warning` and the comment "drop it without a message — the trade is not
today's". The reasoning is right (16 Nov: a PNB entry from 11 Nov produced
`TARGET HIT T1 136.75` against a different day's candles) but the conclusion was
wrong: it is still the user's position, and the scanner stopped following it
without saying so. The test for that behaviour asserted `assertFalse(send.called)`.

`fixed` — one line, once: `⚠ TRADE ABANDONED — BUY PNB (135.73, entered
2026-11-11) was dropped before an exit alert: a trade is intraday, so it is not
trailed against 2026-11-17's candles. Manage it yourself.` The test now asserts
exactly one message, naming the entry date, with no hit alert.

### 2.3 Two entry drops were invisible

* `if ysym not in current: continue` — the pick named a sector, a name in it had
  no published 5m candle, and the scan moved on. Nothing anywhere.
* the early scan's `except Exception: continue` — a name that raised was only in
  the Actions log.

`fixed` — `no_data` and `scan_error` are now reject keys, so they appear in
`Signals not alerted: 1 had no 5m candle to scan (X 09:20) · 1 could not be
scanned (Y 09:25)`.

### 2.4 Bot 3's OI refusal was counted nowhere

Bot 1 has always recorded `oi` in its summary; bot 3 called `_no_projection`
(its own diagnostic) and skipped the reject book, so an OI refusal could not be
seen in the 1m report. `fixed` — one `_note_reject` call; both summaries now list
the same refusals in the same words.

## 3. Checked, and correctly silent

* **A failed send.** Nothing advances the ledger on `send → False`; the block is
  re-sent next cycle and the entry/target/SL/exit are replayed from that candle.
  These are the paths that could double-send, and the ledger key is written
  *after* the send, never before.
* **The same level twice.** `alerts` is keyed `symbol@entry_bar:LEVEL`, so a
  restart re-reads the ledger instead of re-announcing.
* **A bar that closes beyond both T1 and the stop.** T1 is announced first and
  the stop that T1 moved binds from the *next* bar — intrabar order is unknown,
  so the entry bar is not judged against a stop that only exists because T1 was
  reached.
* **Nothing to say.** No fire on the candle, a name already in `sent`/`positions`,
  a WAIT pick: the summary still explains the day (`no stock in the picked
  sectors gave a signal`).
* **A pick that changes while the scan is late** (item 2 above): the change is
  not lost, it is printed with the next cycle's bar. Fixing it would mean sending
  a block stamped with a candle the scanner could no longer judge — worse.

## 4. Two asymmetries between the bots (fixed in 5)

The inventory above is about *whether* a message exists. Reading the two loops
side by side also turns up places where the same message exists on both bots but
under different rules — the failure mode the whole persist test is about.

## 5. Follow-up: the two asymmetries, fixed

### 5.1 A projection that died was silent (bot 3 only)

`SCAN1M_CANCEL_MESSAGE` defaulted to `0`, so a projection that did not survive
the close left nothing behind but a counter in the 15:33 summary. On 17 Nov that
was three REALTY projections, cancelled because the 5m block had dropped the pick
to WAIT while the bar still read REALTY — the user was asked to watch three
trades and never told whether to act on any of them. An unconfirmed heads-up that
quietly disappears is the worst of both worlds.

`fixed` — the default is `1`: one line per died projection, with the projection's
price, the close and the reason (`not checked — the pick dropped to WAIT`).
`SCAN1M_CANCEL_MESSAGE=0` restores silence; the summary counter stays either way.

### 5.2 Bot 3's market block ignored the confidence number

Bot 1 has sent a block on a `MARKET_CONF_DELTA` move since 16 Nov (99% slid to
67% with no message while three entries were taken). Bot 3's copy of the same
block watched only the direction label and the pick — and with
`SCAN1M_MARKET_SOURCE=auto` bot 3 is sometimes the only loop that decides the
market, so a confidence slide could go unannounced on both. `fixed` — the same
three triggers (label, pick+side, confidence) and the same `prev_conf` passed to
`format_market`, so the block can show the move.

## 6. State of the stream after this audit

13 message types, 10 refusal reasons, and three "the day ended like this" lines
(open at the close, not checked, abandoned). Every one of them is either
unconditional, or gated by a knob the `doctor` command prints.

Tests: `AlertInventoryTests` (5) in `tests/test_live_alerts.py`,
`test_a_cancelled_projection_is_reported_by_default` and
`test_a_confidence_slide_is_reported_like_bot_1` in `tests/test_scan1m.py`, and
the abandoned trade's test updated to assert the message. 482 passed, pyflakes
clean.
