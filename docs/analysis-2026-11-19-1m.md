## What changed

Two changes, both on the **coverage** side of the trim-vs-coverage split. Neither
touches the trim (`SECTOR_TOP_N`), the cap, the entry candle or the exit rules.

1. **`LATE_LEADER_ENTRY` (default `1`) — the late leader is a trade, not a note.**
   When the catch-up finds a leader that fired before its sector was picked and
   the trade is *still there* — the fire candle's EMA9 stop untouched since, the
   nearest target still at least `MIN_TARGET_R` (0.5) of the risk taken from
   here, inside the existing 30-minute / 2R gates, and room under
   `MAX_ENTRIES_PER_PICK` for its sector+side — it is entered at the price now
   with the fire candle's stop, and sent as `⏰ LATE LEADER ENTRY`. Otherwise the
   message stays the heads-up.

   The target test is what decides 19 Nov name by name, and it is not the same
   question as "has the price passed T1". M&M was `+2.0R` at 10:00: price 3461.00,
   T1 R2 3462.77, so only 1.77 points of room against the 44-point stop the
   catch-up would take from there — 0.04R. Entering that is paying 44 points for
   1.8. It is refused, and M&M stays a heads-up. VOLTAS (+0.3R) and DIXON
   (+0.4R) still had their targets ahead of them and are the names this rule
   exists for.
   Both bots honour it: bot 1 in `pipeline.take_late_entry` (from
   `_send_late_leaders`), bot 3 in `scan1m._catch_up`.
   `LATE_LEADER_ENTRY=0` restores today's behaviour exactly.

2. **`SECTOR_EXTRA_MAX` (default `1`) — the pick's side is not one sector.**
   A committed pick scanned one sector, so on a committed long bar CDUR could not
   be scanned next to AUTO no matter how strong it was (only the NEUTRAL follow
   scanned more than one). The scan now adds up to one more sector on the pick's
   own side when it passes the same `_is_leading` test the follow uses, and the
   market message names it: `PERSIST + AUTO qualified (score 97); also scanning
   CDUR`. Each extra sector carries its own `MAX_ENTRIES_PER_PICK` budget.
   `SECTOR_EXTRA_MAX=0` = the pick alone.

## Two bugs found in the same log

* **`telegram bot3 rejected the markup (400 … Unsupported start tag "" at byte
  offset 400–457)`** on the early and confirmed messages: the literal `(< 0.5R)`
  in the skipped-pivot line. Fixed before this log was taken (`pipeline._target_text`
  now writes `(under 0.5R)`); the retry as plain text is why every alert that had
  a skipped pivot arrived unformatted today.
* **The summary's two candle books disagreed**: `Candles confirmed 72 of 75`
  beside `⚠ 4 candle(s) never judged: 15:10, 15:15, 15:20, 15:25` — 76 candles in
  a 75-candle session. `candles` counts close passes that *started*, `judged`
  counts those that finished. The line now names both when they differ
  (`Candles confirmed 72 of 75 · with a sector pick 69 · 71 judged`).

## 21 Nov: a count is not a quality filter

`MAX_ENTRIES_PER_PICK = 3` was the wrong instrument. It keeps whichever three
names arrive first on a candle and throws away the fourth runner — and it says
nothing about whether any of the three is actually moving. It is **0 by default
now** (a positive value restores it), and what keeps a name out is its own tape:

| test | default | refuses |
|---|---|---|
| `ENTRY_MIN_RANK` | 40 | no strength, no volume, poor structure: `weak momentum rank 31` |
| reversal veto | on | a close on the wrong side of its EMA9 with the last candles against it: `reversing — under its EMA9 with the last candles against it` |
| `ENTRY_RANK_BAND` | 0.75 | per sector and candle, a name ranked below 75% of that sector's best: `rank 47 is behind its sector's runners` |

Both bots apply all three (5m close pass, 1m confirm pass, and the late-leader
entry), an early alert that would have become one of these is cancelled with the
same words, and every refusal lands in the summary's
`not running — sideways, reversing or weak momentum` count with its reason. The
entry order is the combined rank, so what goes out first is the name that is
moving hardest inside its sector — not the name with the biggest day percentage.
