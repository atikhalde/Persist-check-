## 3. Where the volume came from (three mechanisms, all in the code)

### 3a. The alert layer has no cap; the entry layer does

`MAX_ENTRIES_PER_PICK=3` ranks and caps **entries** (`rank_candidates`,
`entry_cap_room`). Nothing caps **alerts**: `run_early` projects every name in the
scan list that prints a fresh signal on that minute, one message each. At 09:21
the pick was FMCG (12 names) with PSUBK as the `also scanning` sector (12 names),
so 24 names were scanned and **11 fired in the same candle** (6 FMCG + 5 PSUBK).

### 3b. `LATE LEADER` is one message per name, and says it is not an entry

17 messages in 35 minutes, each ending *"Not an entry alert … check the stop
yourself"*. Their own text prices them: at the moment of the alert they were, on
average, **+0.2R from the fire** (range −0.3R … +1.0R) — the move had already
happened. One of the 17 later became an entry (BANKINDIA, on a fresh fire).

### 3c. The alert named the **pick's** sector, not the stock's

`format_projected` / `format_catchup` printed `pick["sector"]` for every name in
the scan list, and that list also holds the `also scanning` sectors. On 17 Nov
that produced:

| alert | printed | actually |
|---|---|---|
| SUNTV, PVRINOX, TIPSMUSIC, ZEEL, HATHWAY, NAZARA, SAREGAMA | `Sector REALTY` | MEDIA |
| BANKBARODA, INDIANB, UNIONBANK | `Sector REALTY` | PSUBK |
| BLUESTARCO | `sector PSUBK` | CDUR |

Ten of the day's alerts misnamed the sector. `config/universe.yaml` is correct —
the label was copied from the pick. **Fixed** in this pass: the alert now prints
the stock's own sector and adds `(scanned with REALTY)` when it came from a
`also scanning` sector, so the message says what it means.

### 3d. The afternoon produced nothing at all — by construction

From 09:50 to the close there were **zero stock alerts**, while the blocks stayed
`STRONG BULL 100% TREND-LOCK` and picked PSUBK 97 → REALTY 96 → BANK 71 → PSUBK 82.
The reason is not a cap: it is that an entry needs a **fresh 5m crossover on that
candle**, and every bank/realty name had already crossed in the first 45 minutes.
`SCAN1M_EARLY_PER_DAY=1` blocks the projection from alerting the same name twice,
and `catchup_candidate` refuses a fire older than `SCAN1M_LEADER_MAX_MINUTES=30`.

So the scanner is front-loaded by design: **all of its signal generation happens
in the opening 45 minutes; all of its trend can happen after 10:00.** Today the
winner (BANKINDIA, +4.1R) was the one name that happened to re-cross after its
sector was picked.

## 4. The other engine disagreed with ours at 12:50

Your indicator's panel that afternoon showed `INDEX DIR LONG ▲ held 49b` together
with `REGIME BEAR ONLY · DEF ▼ → ret`, `FLOW MAP −8.9 STRONG BEARISH` and
`BULL/BEAR BANK 41.8 vs DEF 66.5`.

Our block at 12:50 said `MARKET STRONG BULL conf 100% TREND-LOCK`, picked BANK
long, and printed `Live flow DEF▼ uQ (70)` on the same screen. Nifty went 25,458 →
25,380 over the next 15 minutes.

Our engine has no flow veto: the institutional vote is VIX-only, `live_flow` is
printed and (outside the follow path) ignored. That is why it can print 100% while
the flow map is −8.9 bearish. It cost nothing today — no crossovers, no entries —
but it is the same asymmetry that made 16 Nov unsellable, in the opposite
direction.

## 5. Ranked changes, with what today says about each

**Measured today, no code needed — a repository variable:**

1. `SECTOR_FOLLOW_MIN_PERSIST` **55 → 75**. The follow path picked FMCG 73 at
   09:15 and CDUR 63 at 09:20; both lost (−0.8R, −2.0R). At 75 neither pick
   exists, REALTY 80 still does. Day goes **−2.8R → 0** on those three trades and
   keeps every winner. It also removes the whole 09:21/09:24 projection burst,
   because a pick that does not exist projects nothing.

**Fixed in this pass (display defect, no rule change):**

2. The alert names the stock's own sector, with `(scanned with X)` when it came
   from a follow sector (3c).

**Proposals, not shipped — each changes behaviour and needs measurement:**

3. **Cap and rank the alert layer per candle** (`SCAN1M_ALERT_CAP`, default `0` =
   today). Top 3 by sector lead per candle would have turned 11 messages at 09:21
   into 3, and 24 → about 6 for the morning.
4. **One digest per pick window instead of one `LATE LEADER` per name**
   (`SCAN1M_CATCHUP_DIGEST`, default `0`): 17 messages → 4.
5. **Refuse an entry with no pivot target** (`REQUIRE_TARGET`, default `0`).
   DABUR was taken with `T1 N/A · T2 N/A · T3 N/A` and lost −0.8R. `MIN_TARGET_R`
   already refuses a target that is too close; it does not cover "none at all".
6. **A flow veto on the aligned path** (`FLOW_VETO`, default `0`): when the live
   flow is qualified and on the opposite side to the pick, stand aside. Three
   afternoon picks would have been removed (none produced an entry), so today it
   measures zero — it needs the replay, not this log.
7. **A continuation lane** for a picked sector whose names all fired hours ago
   (a fresh pullback-and-reclaim in the sector, capped like an entry). This is
   the only proposal that addresses the *missed* half of the day, and it cannot
   be judged from alerts alone — it needs `backtest --date 2026-11-17`.

## 6. What "few genuine" actually means here

Not "the scanner picks the wrong sectors" — seven of seven entries were in a
sector that was leading at the time, and the day's sector rotations (FMCG → CDUR →
REALTY → PSUBK → BANK) were read correctly by the block within one candle.

It means **the scanner converts a front-loaded signal burst into a capped number
of entries at the weakest moment of the day** (the open, before the market has a
side), and then spends the rest of the session announcing names whose move has
already happened. Fixing the *entry timing* (1) and the *volume* (3, 4) is worth
more than any new rule: on this day it is −2.8R → 0 on the losses, with no
winner touched.
