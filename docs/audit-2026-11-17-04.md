# Audit — the two 17 Nov summary blocks, read against each other

The 13:05 pair: bot 3's `1m projected scan 2026-11-17` and bot 1's
`Scanner summary 2026-11-17`. Read side by side they agree on the sector for
every one of the 62 candles that had a pick — the standing goal, confirmed from
the report rather than from the alerts. They also disagree in two places, and one
of those disagreements cost bot 3 the best trade of the morning.

## The blocks

|---|---|---|
| candles | 73 · with a sector pick 62 | 73 of 75 · with a sector pick 62 |
| sectors picked | PSUBK 27 · REALTY 26 · BANK 5 · CDUR f2 · FMCG f1 · REALTY f1 = **62** | PSUBK 27 · REALTY 26 · BANK 5 · CDUR f3 · FMCG f2 · REALTY f2 · PSUBK f1 = **66** |

Both agree on **PSUBK 27 · REALTY 26 · BANK 5** and on 62 picked candles, which is
the thing that matters: the two loops picked the same sector on the same candle,
all day. Everything that differs is either a different universe of messages
(projections vs heads-ups) or one of the two bugs below.

## Finding 1 — the sector line had two arithmetics

`Sectors picked (candles)` summed to **66 on bot 1 against 62 picked candles**,
because bot 1 counted the follow sectors inside the pick's book (`pick_sector`
*and* every `extra`), while bot 3 counted the pick alone. Same label, same day,
two numbers. A reader checking the block for consistency would find a 4-candle
hole that does not exist.

```
17 Nov, bot 1 before:  PSUBK 27 · REALTY 26 · BANK 5 · CDUR 3 · FMCG 2 · REALTY 2 · PSUBK 1   (66)
17 Nov, both after:   PSUBK 27 · REALTY 26 · BANK 5 · CDUR long, follow 3
                      · followed: FMCG 2 · REALTY 2 · PSUBK 1                                   (62 + 5)
```

`fixed` — one definition, in `_summary_sectors`, used by both loops: the pick is
counted once per candle, and what it dragged along is a separate clause. Bot 1's
`stats["sectors"]` now holds picked candles only; the extras live in
`stats["extra"]` and are counted by both loops the same way
(`scan1m._count_candle` matches `run_once`).

## Finding 2 — bot 3 charged the cap to the wrong sector, and it cost +2.8R

Bot 3 computed **one** cap key per candle, from the pick, and applied it to every
candidate — including names from the follow sectors:

```python
cap_key = pick_cap_key(pick["sector"], int(pick["side"]))   # once, for the candle
for cand in candidates:
    if not entry_cap_room(st, cap_key): ...
```

Bot 1 has always keyed it per candidate (`pick_cap_key(sector_of[raw], side)`),
with the comment *"one sector is one risk factor — counted per sector, not per
message"*. So on a follow scan bot 3 refused an extra sector's names out of the
pick's budget, and the refusal text named a sector the name does not belong to.

17 Nov says exactly that: bot 3's summary reports **BLUESTARCO 09:20, VOLTAS,
HAVELLS** as *"not taken — the pick already had its entries"* while the pick was
FMCG, and bot 1 — same candle, same cap, keyed correctly — took **BLUESTARCO for
+2.8R**, the best trade of its morning. Bot 3's 7 entries against bot 1's 10 are
the same story in one number.

`fixed` — the key is per candidate (`own_key`), for `entry_cap_room` and for
`take_entry_slot`; the refusal text names the sector the name actually lives in.

## What the blocks say that is not a bug

* **3 of 25 projections became entries (12%).** The projection's value is the
  2.4-minute head start on the three that did, not a batting average — and the 4
  close-only entries are bot 3's own ceiling working, not missed trades.
* **`older than the window 404`** — the late-leader catch-up refuses four out of
  five candidates for being older than `SCAN1M_LEADER_MAX_MINUTES` (30). 17 of
  478 sent is the rule doing its job, not a broken filter.
* **`2408 no signal on the forming candle`** — two-thirds of the name-candles
  never fire; that is the engine's selectivity, and the number is the reason the
  62 picked candles produced 7 entries.
* **The two "earlier" counts (47 vs 31)** differ because bot 3 also books the
  heads-ups it cancelled on a pick change; the definitions are in the same book
  and the same words, the universes are not.
* **`12 cap` on bot 3 vs `9` on bot 1** — after finding 2 this is the sectors
  doing their own arithmetic, and the numbers should move apart less. Worth a
  check on the next session rather than a fix.
