# Audit — 17 Nov 2026, bot 1 (5m scanner), persist test

Same question as `audit-2026-11-17-01.md`, asked of the other scanner: **did the
5m scanner pick the persistent moving stock from its sector, and did every
refusal say why?** The evidence is the full bot-1 stream for 17 Nov — 11 market
blocks, 6 EARLY heads-ups, 10 confirmations, 4 unverified/cancelled heads-ups and
9 management messages, ~40 messages for 10 entries.

Bot 3 (1m) traded the same sectors on the same day for **+3.4R** over 7 entries
(see `-01`). Bot 1 traded 10 for **+4.7R**. Both books are dominated by the same
four names, and both books' losses came from the same three sectors at the same
low persist. The persist test is not a 1m-only effect.

## What went wrong (measured)

### 1. A NEUTRAL follow scans two sectors, each with its own cap — 6 entries on one candle

09:25 printed six entry blocks on **one** candle: three FMCG (73) and three CDUR
(65), under `MARKET NEUTRAL conf 49% WEAK` with `SECTOR FOLLOW`. `pick_entries`
is keyed `sector side`, and the cap (3) was honoured on both sectors — the cap is
not leaking. The leak is upstream: `_follow_fallback` returns every leader past
the first as `extra_sectors`, so a NEUTRAL bar trades *n* sectors × 3 names. That
is how one 49%-confident candle produced 60% of the day's entries and all four of
its losses.

Measured, this is **not** worth a gate: dropping the extras on 17 Nov removes the
PGEL and CROMPTON losses (−2.0R) *and* the BLUESTARCO winner (+2.8R), i.e. the
day gets 0.4R **worse**. Listed as a proposal, not a fix.

### 2. Two of ten entries had no target at all

HINDUNILVR printed `T2 N/A · T3 N/A`; DABUR printed `T1 N/A · T2 N/A · T3 N/A`
and was still entered. Bot 3 has refused target-less entries since 17 Nov
(`SCAN1M_REQUIRE_TARGET`); bot 1 has no equivalent. Both names lost (−1.4R),
which is the shape of a trade with no defined exit rather than a verdict.

### 3. The pick flipped inside one 5m candle, and the line said nothing

* 09:40 picked REALTY; the three 1m projections on REALTY were cancelled with
  "pick dropped to WAIT" while the 5m bar itself still read REALTY.
* 09:45 printed **two market blocks five seconds apart**: the first
  `PERSIST + REALTY qualified (score 96) · held — PSUBK 97 is +1.0, needs 5 to
  take the pick`, the second `PERSIST + PSUBK qualified (score 97)`.

Mechanism: `previous_pick` requires the ledger slot to hold `bar − 5m`. The early
(pre-close) pass writes its verdict for the forming candle into that same slot,
so when the close pass re-evaluates the *same* candle the slot holds `bar`, the
`was != now − 5m` test fails, and the incumbent is dropped — `PICK_STICKY_MARGIN`
silently stops applying, so the pick is decided on raw score. It is the one place
where the same candle can legally produce two different answers.

Making it deterministic would have **held REALTY** at 97 vs 96 and therefore lost
BANKINDIA (+4.1R), the best trade of the day. So the behaviour was not changed on
one day of alerts. What shipped is the audit trail: the pick line now carries
`· REALTY not held — this candle's pick was already written, so the candle
before it is gone`. A flip that says nothing is indistinguishable from a bug.

### 4. Checked and not a defect

* **13:05–14:00** picked persistent sectors all afternoon (BANK 58–60, PSUBK
  82–86) and entered nothing: no fresh crossover in those sectors. Correct.
* **12:50** picked BANK 71 with a qualified `DEF▼ Q (70)` live flow against it;
  the `· live flow against (DEF)` text shipped on 17 Nov (from the 1m round) and
  covers this. The bar landed after the close — no trade either way.
* **Message volume**: ~40 messages for 10 entries. The 09:25 candle alone is
  six heads-ups plus six confirmations; the rest of the day is one block per
  candle. The `SCAN1M_ALERT_CAP`/digest work does not apply — bot 1's cap is per
  sector and behaved.

## Fixed (this round)

1. `fixed (no knob, text only)` — **the sticky lapse is named on the pick line.**
   `market.lapsed_stickiness` + a `sticky_note` on `run_market`; the note is only
   appended when a pick existed on this candle's ledger slot and could not be
   used as an incumbent. Behaviour, pick, entries and R are unchanged — this is
   the "every refusal is auditable" half of defect 3, shipped without the
   unmeasured half. Tests: `StickyLapseNoteTests` (4) in
   `tests/test_market_direction.py`, `StickyLapseOnThePickLineTests` (2) in
   `tests/test_sector_pick.py`.

## Proposals (need a replay, not one day of alerts)

2. `proposal` — **hold the pick across a same-candle re-evaluation**
   (`PICK_STICKY_LOG`): key the incumbent by bar in the ledger instead of one
   slot. Measured on 17 Nov: −4.1R. Replay `backtest --date 2026-11-17` plus the
   12–16 Nov sessions before shipping.
3. `proposal` — **`SCAN5M_REQUIRE_TARGET`**: refuse a 5m entry with no T1, the way
   bot 3 does. 2 of 10 entries on 17 Nov, −1.4R, and both had no exit rule. Needs
   a replay to see how many *winners* it would have dropped.
4. `proposal` — **`SECTOR_FOLLOW_EXTRAS`**: one sector per NEUTRAL follow.
   Measured −0.4R on 17 Nov (removes −2.0R of losses and +2.8R of profit), so it
   is not the fix for defect 1; the fix there is likely "no follow on a 49%
   tape", which is the `SECTOR_FOLLOW_ON_WEAK` question and needs the same
   replay.
5. `proposal` (carried from `-01`) — `FLOW_VETO`, `DIVERGE_SPREAD=2`,
   `PICK_WEAK_ALIGNED_MIN_PERSIST`: unchanged, still waiting on a replay.

Nothing in this audit changes the shipped 1m behaviour in `6690b81`.
