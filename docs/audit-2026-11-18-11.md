# 18 Nov — the 1m bot's afternoon (run #121, 14:12–15:33 IST)

The 1m session's last run of the day: guard clean, `scan` 14:12:24 → 15:33:09 (80 m 45 s),
`1m live loop ended: closed`. Two things were wrong, and both were silent — that is the whole
point of this note, because the afternoon itself was uneventful.

## The afternoon, briefly

* **market** MET short, announced at 14:15 and again at 15:10 (`BEAR / MET`);
* **no projections**: the twelve MET names all fired on candles from 09:20 to 11:35, hours before
  the pick — the round-robin checked every one of them each minute and refused every one as
  `earlier`;
* **two alerts lost** to the same bare `<` as the morning (see below), everything else delivered;
* no entries, no position events, no exceptions.

## Finding 1 — the index feed stopped at 15:15 and two closes were never judged

```
15:20:08  1m 15:15: 11 of 12 feeds had not published — will retry
15:21:08  1m 15:15: 10 of 12 feeds had not published — will retry
15:25:07  1m 15:20 close: pick not available yet — will retry
15:26:07  1m 15:20 close: pick not available yet — will retry
15:27:07  1m 15:20 close: pick not available yet — will retry
          … 15:28: the three-minute alert deadline expires; the 15:20 close is never judged
15:30:07  1m 15:25 close: pick not available yet — will retry
15:31:07  1m 15:25 close: pick not available yet — will retry
15:32:07  1m 15:25 close: pick not available yet — will retry
15:33:07  1m live loop ended: closed      ← the 15:25 close is still owed
```

The Nifty 5m feed published its last bar for the day at **15:15**. Bot 3 cannot judge a close
without the market bar for that candle, so it kept saying "pick not available yet — will retry"
until the candle's three-minute deadline (`MAX_ALERT_LAG_SECONDS = 180`) ran out — and then
simply stopped asking. The loop exited at 15:33 with the last candle of the day still owed.
The 15:15 close was in the same position a minute earlier — ten of its twelve feeds were still
missing at 15:22, and its own three minutes ran out at 15:23 — so the day may have ended with
three candles unjudged, not two. Neither the log nor the summary said a word about any of them:
the summary's only trace is a *"Candles confirmed N of 75"* count, and N went unexplained.

Now every judged candle is written to a book (`stats["judged"]`) and the session's end names the
rest:

```
WARNING nse_scanner.scan1m: 1m candle(s) never judged: 15:20, 15:25 — no market bar for them
inside the alert deadline, so their entries were not considered
```

and the summary carries the same line:

```
⚠ candle(s) never judged: 2 — 15:20, 15:25 — no market bar for them inside the alert deadline,
  so their entries were not considered
```

That is the honest counterpart of bot 1's *"no NSE bar for 09:35 after 120s of polling — leaving
this candle"* (#47): a candle that could not be judged is now a named candle, not a smaller number.

## Finding 2 — the summary was sent (or not) without a trace

`_end_of_session` sent the 15:33 summary through `send_bot3` and closed the run. If Telegram had
refused it — and seven messages *were* refused that afternoon — the only evidence would have been
an exception in a `try` block, and `reported` would have stayed unset in a ledger that no later
run of the day ever reads (the guard skips the scan job after 15:33). In other words: a lost
summary was unrecoverable and undetectable.

Both bots now say which happened:

```
INFO  nse_scanner.scan1m: 1m summary sent: 71 candles, 14 lines
ERROR nse_scanner.scan1m: 1m summary was NOT delivered — Telegram refused it; the session is
                          over, so no later run will send it
```

and the same two lines for bot 1 (`pipeline.live_loop`), whose summary was equally silent.
