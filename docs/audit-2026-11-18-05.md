# 18 Nov — the 1m summary, audited

The day's own report from bot 3, checked line by line against the code that writes it.

```
📊 1m projected scan 2026-11-18
Candles confirmed 73 · with a sector pick 57
Sectors picked (candles): CDUR short 50 · MET short 7
Projected alerts 2 · of which confirmed at the close 0 · did not hold 2
Average lead 3.5 min before the 5m close (earliest 3 min)
LATE LEADER catch-ups 7 (signal fired before its sector was picked) · not sent: already ran (chase) 2 · older than the window 48 · no earlier fire 12
Checked, not projected: no signal on the forming candle 2513 · 1m feed missing/stale 72
Entries (close-confirmed, sent to this bot) 0
Signals not alerted: 20 fired before their sector was picked (CROMPTON 09:25, DIXON 09:25, HAVELLS 09:25, KALYANKJIL 09:20, TITAN 09:25, VOLTAS 09:25 …) · 3 on the opposite side to the pick (AMBER 09:50, BLUESTARCO 09:50, PGEL 09:50)
Open at the close: none — every entry got its exit alert
Projected alerts fire 1–4 minutes before the 5m close.
```

## Finding 1 — the report contradicted itself, twice on one screen

**Seven names were alerted and reported as not alerted.** The first line counts
`LATE LEADER catch-ups 7`; the reject line then lists `20 fired before their sector was
picked` — and the names it shows, `KALYANKJIL 09:20`, `TITAN 09:25`, `CROMPTON 09:25`,
`DIXON 09:25`, `HAVELLS 09:25`, `VOLTAS 09:25`, are exactly the seven names the bot had
already reported as labelled heads-ups that morning.

`_summary_rejects` dropped a name from that list only if it was in `sent` (an *entry*). A
catch-up is not an entry — by design it writes nothing to `sent`/`positions` — so the same
name appeared in both books. Fixed: the "fired before their sector was picked" line now
excludes names in the catch-up book as well as in `sent`, in **both** bots (bot 3 imports the
function, bot 1 calls it directly), so the day reads `13` and names only what was never
reported:

```
Signals not alerted: 13 fired before their sector was picked (AMBER 09:25, BATAINDIA 09:25, …) · 3 on the opposite side to the pick (…)
```

**Two units in one report.** `older than the window 48` (checks) sat next to `20 fired before
their sector was picked` (names): each catch-up window re-checks a name on every minute of the
forming candle, so one name can be skipped as many times as it is checked. Unlabelled, 48 > 20
looks like an error. The clause now says what it counts:

```
· not sent (per name-check): already ran (chase) 2 · older than the window 48 · no earlier fire 12
```

Both lines are now reconcilable: 13 names never reported + 7 reported = the 20 the reject book
held, and 62 name-checks explain *why* those 13 were not reported.

## Finding 2 — the header did not match bot 1's

`Candles confirmed 73` against bot 1's `Candles scanned 73 of 75`. The total is the context that
makes 73 meaningful (two candles of the session were not judged). Bot 3 now prints
`Candles confirmed 73 of 75 · with a sector pick 57` — same shape, same number, different verb
because it counts a different thing (candles the close pass judged).

## Finding 3 — a zero-entry day promised exits for trades that never existed

`Open at the close: none — every entry got its exit alert`. On 18 Nov there were **0 entries**:
the sentence is vacuously true and reads as if trades had been managed. It now says
`none — no entry was opened today` when the day's entry count is zero, and keeps the original
line when there were entries and all of them were closed out.

## What the day proves about the earlier audits

A sector picked for **50 consecutive candles** produced **zero entries** in both bots, and two
projections in total — both on CDUR names that fired a *new* crossover after the pick
(10:16, 10:52), both refused at their close. That is `audit-2026-11-18-01/02` in the day's own
numbers: the engine enters on a crossover, not on persistence. Nothing in this summary is
missing; the mechanism is what it is, and it is now reported instead of silent.
