## 4. Was picking AUTO in the morning wrong?

**Yes — but not because AUTO was the wrong sector at 09:15. Because at 09:15
nothing was knowable, and the score said otherwise.**

AUTO's persist was 92 with a **+4.29% day move and +0.37% of it made after the
open**. The engine was scoring a gap. AUTO then gave back 3.77pp of that gap
across the session (day +4.29% → +0.52%) while its intraday move only ever
reached +1.01%. The two entries were taken into that fade: TMPV −0.91R, MARUTI
still open.

The counterfactual matters, though, and it is uncomfortable: refusing a
gap-dominated pick at 09:15 would have handed the pick to **FMCG** (persist 73,
day +1.73%, intra +0.15%), and FMCG finished the day at **−0.21%** — worse than
AUTO. So a "gap veto" is not the edge; it just changes which coin comes up. The
honest conclusion is that the *first* pick of a session is close to noise, and a
rule that pretends to choose better at 09:15 is fitting one day's tape.

What the morning did produce, and can be fixed, is the **false alerts**: five
early heads-ups, four of them on the 09:20 candle (BAJAJ-AUTO, MARUTI, UNOMINDA,
TMPV). Two were cancelled at the close — BAJAJ-AUTO ("rank 60 is behind its
sector's runners") and UNOMINDA ("the closed candle fired the opposite side") —
one stopped, one survived. A heads-up is advisory: the close confirm books the
trade, so holding back the earliest ones cannot lose an entry. On the two days I
can measure, bot 1 did worse: **six early alerts across 18–19 Nov, six
cancelled** (TCS, INFY, TECHM, IOB, PSB, BATAINDIA).

## 5. The edge, in the order the evidence supports it

### 5.1 A pick that arrives late must re-open the entry window  (shipped: `PICK_CATCHUP=1`)

BANK + FIN = **+2.50% and +2.04%** on the day, **zero** trades, 81 catch-ups
refused as "too old", 38 candles of BANK picked with nothing buyable. The 40-minute
clock measures the fire, but the thing that makes a name tradeable is the *pick*
— and the pick can arrive four hours later. The anti-chase guards (the fire's own
stop untouched since, ≤ `SCAN1M_LEADER_MAX_R` = 2R from the fire) are what actually
stop a chase; the clock was a proxy for them and it was measuring the wrong event.

**Estimate.** BANK's index made +1.03pp after the 12:05 pick; AXISBANK added
another ~1.0pp of relative strength on top of it (lead +1.60 → +2.60). At
₹100,000 a trade that is ≈ +2% ≈ ₹2,000 gross per name, minus ~₹100 of costs —
roughly **+1.8R per name**, two or three names. Against a closed book of −₹1,008,
this is the difference between a red day and a green one.

### 5.2 A name above every pivot is a leader, not a reject  (shipped: `TARGET_FALLBACK=1`)

Five of IT's top five were refused for "no pivot target" at 09:30 and the bot
bought ranks 8+ instead. Every exit in the whole book was the EMA9 trail, so the
target gate has never once shaped an exit — it has only removed names. The
fallback gives such a name a *measured* target at the same floor every pivot must
clear (`MIN_TARGET_R` / `MIN_TARGET_PCT`), so the trade is still defined and the
entry, the stop and the risk are unchanged.

**Estimate.** Today's five refused names were ranked 1st–5th in a sector that ran
+2.35% → +3.46% (peak 10:40) before fading to +1.63%. Held to the EMA9 trail they
would have returned somewhere between the two IT trades that did work (+0.33R)
and flat; the point is that the *ranking* would have been right, which is what a
dozen days of tape is needed to price properly.

### 5.3 Do not send a heads-up on the first two candles  (shipped: `SCAN1M_EARLY_FROM=09:30`)

4 of 4 early alerts on the 09:20 candle were cancelled or stopped, and the two
entries they fronted were booked by the close pass anyway. Bot 1's equivalent
feature was 6-for-6 cancelled on 18–19 Nov. A projection is advisory; suppressing
the earliest ones costs no entry and removes the alerts the user learns to ignore.

### 5.4 What is *not* shipped, and why

| candidate | evidence | why it waits |
|---|---|---|
| Gap veto on the pick | AUTO was a +3.92pp gap and faded 3.77pp | the replacement pick (FMCG) finished **worse**; a veto is fitting one tape |
| No entries in the first candle | TMPV/MARUTI at 09:20 lost/faded | 11 Nov's best entries of the day were at 09:20 (+4.3R, +11.5R) |
| Kill bot 1's early alerts | 6/6 cancelled on 18–19 Nov | two days is not a sample; measure it with `EARLY_*` counts first |
| Hold winners past EMA9 | every exit was the trail; TECHM gave back +0.41R → +0.33R | needs the MFE per trade, which the ledger does not record yet |

## 6. What was fixed, so the next audit can prove it

| change | knob | why |
|---|---|---|
| Pick-change catch-up | `PICK_CATCHUP=1` | the BANK/FIN hole; the anti-chase guards are unchanged |
| Measured target for a broken-out name | `TARGET_FALLBACK=1` | five IT leaders refused at 09:30 for "no exit" |
| Early heads-up only after 09:30 | `SCAN1M_EARLY_FROM=09:30` | 4 of 4 first-candle heads-ups were bad; no entry is lost |
| Audit day % for bot 3 | — | every sector read `0.00%` because bot 3 keeps no histogram; the tape has it |
| Risk ₹ and return on risk | — | the closed record did not store `risk`, so "risk deployed" was always ₹0 |
| Report names the refusal | — | "81 leader catch-ups refused as older than the window (PICK_CATCHUP)" is now a line in *What to look at tomorrow* |

Three of these are measurement fixes. They matter more than they look: without
them this analysis could not have been written from the audit alone, and the two
money bugs (day % = 0, risk ₹ = 0) made the report look like a day in which no
sector moved and no risk was taken.

## 7. What to watch tomorrow

1. **`catchup_skip.old`** — if it is large again, the pick is arriving after the
   names have fired, and `PICK_CATCHUP` should have converted some of it into
   entries. Compare *entries in the picked sector* against *candles picked*.
2. **Entries vs the ranked board.** For each entry, where did its name sit in its
   sector's top five on that candle? Today: 3 of 6 entries were outside the top
   eight. That ratio is the single best measure of whether the entry filter is
   picking leaders or leftovers.
3. **Costs as a share of gross.** 96% today. Anything above ~35% means the book is
   trading noise — fewer, bigger, or better-timed trades, not more of them.
4. **`unjudged` candles.** Seven today, three inside the winning sector's window.
   No rule can trade a candle that was never judged.
