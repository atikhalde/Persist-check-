# 18 Nov — one opening range, 25 minutes, for both sides

The per-side split (`docs/audit-2026-11-18-12.md`) is reverted. There is one opening range again,
and it is **25 minutes**: `OR_MINUTES = 25`, read by the long side and the sell side alike. Nothing
else changed — not an entry window, not a threshold, not a knob, not a line of alert text.

## What 25 means on a 5-minute grid

`market_open_minutes` is 0 at 09:15, so the gate `mom >= 25` first holds on the **09:40** bar, and the
bars that build the range are the five from 09:15 to 09:35 inclusive (09:35 is `mom = 20`, still
inside). The 09:40 bar is therefore the first bar a pick can be committed on, judged by the 5m loop at
09:45.

| range | bars that build it | first bar a pick can be committed on |
|---|---|---|
| 30 (before) | 09:15 – 09:40 | 09:45 |
| 15 (split, sell side only) | 09:15 – 09:30 | 09:30 |
| **25 (now, both sides)** | **09:15 – 09:35** | **09:40** |

So compared with the 30 it replaces, this is **exactly one candle earlier for both sides** — the buy
signals move from 09:45 to 09:40 and the sell signals move from 09:45 to 09:40 too. It is not a
halfway house in any other sense: the two sides are back on the same clock, and the range is a single
high/low pair again.

## What changed in the code

* `session.OR_MINUTES = 25`; `OR_MINUTES_SHORT` is gone, and nothing references it.
* `market.OpeningRange` is back to one range: `hi`, `lo`, `extend()`, `ready()` returning a single
  boolean, `reset()` per session. Both call sites (`run_market` for the direction, `_sector_snap` for
  the early-rotation boost) read the same gate — `or_up` on the range high, `or_dn` on the range low,
  early bull through the high, early bear through the low.
* The seeding rule is unchanged: the index engine seeds a range whose window it missed (a scan that
  starts at 10:00 still gets a number), the sector scorer does not (it must never report an early
  rotation off a range it never watched form).

Everything else is as it was in `9ac82ea`: entry windows, EMA9 stop, exit, `PICK_MIN_CONF`,
`_dir_conf`, the OI gate, the entry cap, the 1m projections, every knob, every alert string. The 1m
bot inherits the 25 through the same `run_market` snapshot without a line of its own changing. The
`or-compare` harness stays deleted.

## What it costs

One candle of head start is a shallower line than 30 minutes: a break of the 09:15–09:35 range happens
on 25 minutes of evidence instead of 30, and the early-rotation boost (8 points of sector score) can
now be earned from the 09:40 bar instead of the 09:45 one. That is a small widening of the door, and
it applies to the long side as well as the short — which is the difference from the reverted split.
