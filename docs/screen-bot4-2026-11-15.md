# Bot 4 — the name-first screen (built 15 Nov 2026)

Bot 4 is a **new, separate** scanner next to bots 1/2 (5m entries) and bot 3 (1m
projections). It does not enter, does not trail, does not touch bot 1's ledger, and
does not send to bots 1/2/3's chats. Each candle it screens **every name of every
sector, both sides**, and surfaces at most a handful of setups **for a human to
choose from** — with entry, stop, T1–T3 and the numbers behind each one.

## Why it exists (the numbers)

The 5m engine can only enter a name whose crossover fired **on the candle it is
judging**. When the pick arrives after its sector's leaders have already fired, that
whole book becomes unenterable for the rest of the session:

| session | sector | candles judged | `historical/wrong-side` refusals | entries |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 Nov | CDUR | 47 | **452** (12 names × 44–46) | 0 |
| 19 Nov | AUTO | 61 | **871** (12 names × 60–61) | 1 (+5.7R) |
| 20 Nov 5m | IT | 32 | 122 (+315 follower trims) | 7 |

Meanwhile the *state* of those names was tradeable. Persistence at entry split 17 Nov
cleanly: `≥ 80` → 4 trades, 4 T1 hits, **+6.2R**; `≤ 73` → 3 trades, no T1, **−2.8R**.
20 Nov: structure 0–60 measured −0.04pp forward with a 6% hit rate vs +0.22pp / 25%
above 60; `|lead| ≥ 3pp` measured −0.08pp; the board's own score at 80–101 was the
*worst* bucket (+0.08pp). Those four numbers are the four gates.

## The rule, in one paragraph

A name is a **setup** when its sector's persistence is `≥ 78` **and not falling**, the
sector's intraday move agrees with the side being screened, the name is still at/near
its own session extreme with a positive 15-minute move on that side, its structure is
`≥ 60`, its strength `≥ 55`, its lead is inside `±2pp`, its own run is `≤ 5R`, and its
plan (entry = this candle's close, stop = EMA9, T1–T3 from the pivots) produces a
positive risk. A sector against a committed market needs persistence `≥ 90` — 19 Nov's
AUTO book (persist 98, +2.60%) was strong enough, and its +5.7R was the day.

Then the survivors are **ranked**, not all sent:

```
score = 0.30·strength + 0.20·persist + 0.15·slope + 0.15·structure
      + 0.10·volume + 0.10·alignment − 0.10·run
```

and at most `SCREEN_MAX_DAY` (6) go out, at most 2 per sector, one per name, nothing
after 14:30, nothing below score 55. The card says **"Not an entry alert"** in so many
words.

## Knobs (all forwarded by both workflows, all in `knobs.AUDITED`)

| knob | default | decides |
|---|---|---|
| `SCREEN_BOT` | `1` | run the screen alongside the 5m scan |
| `SCREEN_MAX_DAY` | `6` | setups surfaced per session |
| `SCREEN_MIN_SCORE` | `55` | floor of the ranking |
| `SCREEN_PERSIST_MIN` | `78` | sector persistence needed to look at its book |
| `SCREEN_SLOPE_MIN` | `0` | persistence change over `SCREEN_SLOPE_BARS` (0 = not falling) |
| `SCREEN_SLOPE_BARS` | `3` | look-back for the slope |
| `SCREEN_STRUCTURE_MIN` | `60` | below this a name is drifting |
| `SCREEN_STRENGTH_MIN` | `55` | the tail of a move is 45–64 |
| `SCREEN_LEAD_MAX` | `2.0` | \|lead\| band in pp |
| `SCREEN_RUN_MAX_R` | `5.0` | how far the name may already have run, in its own R |
| `SCREEN_NEAR_HIGH_PCT` | `0.20` | how close to its own extreme counts |
| `SCREEN_COUNTER_PERSIST` | `90` | persistence needed to screen against the market |
| `SCREEN_CAP_PER_SECTOR` | `2` | one sector is one risk factor |
| `SCREEN_COOLDOWN_MIN` | `45` | no name re-surfaced inside this (and never twice a day) |
| `SCREEN_LAST_ENTRY` | `14:30` | no setup after this |

## Wiring

* **Hook**: `pipeline.run_once` → right after `board.sweep`, on the same closed candle
  and the same frames (`board.last_frames()`), `_screen_candle()` runs
  `screen.consider(...)`, sends each setup with `telegram.send_bot4`, then sends any
  state change with `screen.updates(...)`. A failure is logged and never costs a candle.
* **State**: `state/screen_state.json` — its own file, its own loader. Bot 1 fails
  *closed* on a corrupt ledger (a duplicate entry is worse than silence); bot 4 fails
  *open* (its loss is an audit, not a trade).
* **EOD**: at 15:33, after the 5m report, `_screen_report()` builds the audit PDF,
  uploads it to bot 4 with a one-screen caption, and marks the session reported so a
  restarted job cannot send it twice. A missed send can be re-run:
  `python -m nse_scanner screen-pdf`.
* **Secrets to add**: `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN_4`, `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID_4`. Without them the
  screen logs and prints instead of sending — it never falls back to bots 1/2.

## The audit PDF (`reports/screen-<session>.pdf`)

1. verdict + scorecard + **config hash** (two sessions with the same hash ran the same rules)
2. the knobs in force, **frozen at the open** (a knob changed mid-session cannot re-label the day)
3. the frozen 20 Nov holdout (must take ≥ 2 of AXISBANK/INDUSINDBK/PERSISTENT/COFORGE/GODREJPROP, none of WIPRO/HCLTECH/TCS)
4. the short list, one row per setup (levels, legs, lead, run, MFE/MAE, 1R, stop, mark, R)
5. one card per setup, including the "why" the user was given
6. the candle-by-candle scan: qualified count, best score, top names, **refusals on that candle**
7. the market tape
8. the sector tape (persistence sampled across the day, side, slope now)
9. **every gate, its knob, and the number that refused it**
10. what the day could not see (stale feeds, no back-fill, nothing traded)

Gates are evaluated cheapest-first and a name is counted against the **first** gate it
fails — that is the number that actually decided it. A gate that refused nothing is as
informative as one that refused everything.

Without `reportlab` installed the same audit is written as a complete `.txt`; the loop
never breaks over its own report.

## Commands

```bash
python -m nse_scanner screen        # one bot-4 pass on the last closed candle
python -m nse_scanner screen-pdf    # re-send today's audit PDF (or --session YYYY-MM-DD)
python -m nse_scanner doctor        # prints bot 4's credentials and every gate value
```

## Tests

`tests/test_screen.py` (42 tests): both sides with mirrored levels, every gate and its
refusal count, the daily/sector caps and the cooldown, the last-entry cut-off, a bot-4
refusal leaving the setup unsent, tracking from the setup's **own** bar (not the day's
high), stop-wins-on-a-both-touched candle, one-shot updates, the HTML contract (no bare
`<`), PDF smoke test, the `.txt` degradation, and bot 4's credentials never falling back
to bots 1/2.
