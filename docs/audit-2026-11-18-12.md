# 18 Nov — one opening range per side: 30 minutes to buy, 15 minutes to sell

> **Superseded** an hour later by `docs/audit-2026-11-18-13.md`: the split is reverted
> and both sides read one 25-minute range. Kept because the reasoning below is what the
> 25 was measured against.

The rule change you asked for, and the only rule that changed: **the buy side keeps the thirty-minute
opening range (09:15–09:45), the sell side now reads a fifteen-minute one (09:15–09:30).** Nothing
else moved — not an entry window, not a threshold, not a knob, not a line of alert text.

The comparison harness from earlier today (`or-compare`, the workflow, its tests and its doc) is
**removed**, as asked. The measuring is finished; the conclusion is in the code.

## What changed, precisely

| | buy side | sell side |
|---|---|---|
| range window | 09:15 – 09:45 | **09:15 – 09:30** |
| first bar a pick can be committed on | 09:45 | **09:30** |
| constant | `OR_MINUTES = 30` | **`OR_MINUTES_SHORT = 15`** |

Two places read the range, and both now read one range per side:

* `market.run_market` — `or_up` (the buy break) is tested against the 30-minute high and is gated on
  the buy side being ready; `or_dn` (the sell break) is tested against the **15-minute low** and is
  gated on the sell side being ready. Both flags still feed the same `_dir_conf` boost.
* `market._sector_snap` — the early-rotation boost for a bull needs the close through the 30-minute
  high; for a bear it needs the close through the **15-minute low**. The rotation window itself
  (`EARLY_ROT_UNTIL`, 11:30) is unchanged; what changes is when the sell side has a range to break.

The range bookkeeping moved into one small class, `market.OpeningRange`, so the two windows cannot
drift apart again: `extend()` widens each range only inside its own window, `ready()` answers the two
gates, and `reset()` clears it at a session boundary. Behaviour is unchanged for the buy side bar for
bar — the same 09:15–09:45 high and low, the same 09:45 readiness, the same seeding when a scan
starts late.

## What this means for a session

Between 09:30 and 09:45 the engine can now **commit and pick a short** while it still cannot pick a
long. That is the whole intent: 18 Nov's misses were shorts fired between 09:20 and 09:40 (DIXON and
TITAN at 09:25, KALYANKJIL at 09:20, the rest of the CDUR block by 09:40), and the pick that would
have scanned them did not exist until 09:45.

It does **not** make those fires entry-eligible by itself. A fire still has to land on a candle whose
pick is live, the sector still has to be picked and qualified, and the OI gate, the entry cap and the
1m projections are untouched. Shorts keep the same stop, the same exit and the same windows.

## What it costs, honestly

A fifteen-minute range is a shallower line than a thirty-minute one, so a bear break of it happens
earlier and on less evidence: more of the opening noise counts as a break, and the early-rotation
boost (8 points of sector score) can now be earned from 09:30. Expect more short candidates in the
first quarter hour, and expect some of them to be the shake-out that a thirty-minute range would have
filtered out. That is the trade you chose, and it is measurable: the `or-compare` harness can be
restored from PR #53's parent if you ever want the numbers again.

## Not changed, and why

* **The buy side.** 30 minutes, 09:45, bar for bar. The move-assisted longs (PNB, SBIN, ABREL) are
  read off the full range and are untouched.
* **The 1m bot.** It reads the same `run_market` snapshot, so it inherits the per-side gates without
  a line of its own changing — a projected short can now appear from 09:30, a projected long still
  from 09:45.
* **`backtest`'s "after 09:45" tables.** They are a leftover of the single-range engine and start at
  09:45; a short pick made between 09:30 and 09:45 will not appear in that diagnostic's dominant-pick
  line. Offline diagnostic only — no alert reads it.
