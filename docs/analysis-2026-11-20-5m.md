# The 5-minute scanner, 20 Nov 2026 — every trade, every miss, and the edge

Tape: 72 candles, 09:15–15:10 IST. Market **STRONG BULL 97 → 100** from 09:45 and
`TREND UP`; the index never gave the day back. The 5m bot made money — **+₹1,757.82
net, +1.41R, 4W/3L** — and it was still the wrong day's trade: it bought the
followers of the sector it picked and refused every leader that led it.

This is the accounting, then the one rule that turns the second half of the tape
into a trade.

---

## 1. The answer in five lines

1. **The pick was right, the entries were the tail of it.** Six of the seven
   entries were board position **#3–#6** in their sector, with strength 45–64 and
   volume/structure already maxed. Those are the names arriving last.
2. **The best names were found and then thrown away by our own gates.**
   COFORGE, PERSISTENT, MPHASIS, LTIM, COFORGE — the four IT leaders the early
   scan flagged at 09:30 and 09:55 — were each cancelled `no pivot target`. They
   then ran another **+2.5pp, +2.8pp, +0.9pp and +1.4pp**.
3. **BANK, the day's strongest sector (+2.50% day, +3.18% intra), produced zero
   trades in 37 candles as the pick.** All five kept names were
   `SKIP historical/wrong-side` from the first candle to the last: they had all
   fired at 09:20, before the sector qualified at 11:50.
4. **That is not a filter being careful — it is 148 refusals of the same four
   names on 37 consecutive candles** (37 × 4), against a sector that kept going:
   after the scan finally reached it, AXISBANK **+1.73pp** and INDUSINDBK
   **+1.03pp** were still available.
5. **The edge is to stop asking for the fire.** When a sector becomes tradeable
   for the first time that day, take its top two names at *that candle's* close
   with *that candle's* stop. On this tape that is **+5.9R** across BANK, FIN and
   REALTY instead of the +0.00R they produced. Shipped as
   `LATE_LEADER_REBASE=1`.

## 5. The 09:20 AUTO call

**The user's verdict is half right, and the half that is wrong matters.**

At 09:15 the persistence floor (70) was met by exactly one sector: **AUTO 85**,
with MET 72 and IT 71 below it and everything else under. The bot did not choose
AUTO over IT — AUTO was the only sector it was allowed to choose. That is the
gate working as written.

What was wrong is everything after the pick:

* AUTO was **+0.37% intra** at 09:15 and its two best names were entered at #1
  and #2 with day move already **+4.15%**. Both entries were at the *end* of a
  gap, not the start of one: TMPV −0.91R, MARUTI +0.89R, net −₹167 over the pair.
* The sector closed **+0.52%** — the 6th of 8 strong sectors, against BANK
  +2.50% and FIN +2.04%. The pick was right for two candles and the bot left at
  09:25 (`AUTO → IT`, persist 87 vs 72). That switch is the best decision of the
  morning and it should have happened at 09:20.
* The telling number: **MARUTI was held from 09:20 to 12:05 for +0.89R** while
  the sector that had actually been picked at 12:05 was never traded at all.
  The capital was in the right place at the wrong time, twice.

So: not "the AUTO pick was the wrong decision" — at 09:15 there was no
alternative. It was the wrong *sizing*: two entries on a two-candle-old pick of
the weakest qualifying sector. The 09:20 pair is where a `MAX_ENTRIES_PER_PICK`
of 1 would have saved ₹980 on TMPV and kept MARUTI's +₹813.

## 6. Why BANK was ignored, and why the last fix did not help

The mechanical chain, exactly as the logs read it:

1. Every bank fired its ENTRY **at 09:20**, five hours before the sector was
   picked (BANK crossed the 70 floor at 11:50, was picked at 12:05–12:10 after a
   dead tie with IT at 12:05).
2. The entry loop only takes a fire *on the decision candle*. A name whose fire
   is earlier is counted `earlier` and forgotten — 35 of them, the whole BANK
   book plus the IT names that had already fired.
3. `PICK_CATCHUP` (shipped 21 Nov) was written for precisely this: a late pick
   re-opens the 40-minute catch-up window. On 20 Nov it did **not** fire, because
   the marker it compares against is stored on the pick-change candle and the
   scanner's own pick record flipped to BANK one candle before the audit's
   timeline does (12:05 vs 12:10). `pick_is_fresh` compared `12:05` with `12:10`,
   said no, and every BANK name fell through to the plain clock.
4. The result is in the audit's own counter: **`catchup_skip: {"old": 148}`** —
   37 BANK candles × 4 names, five kept names in the book on every one of them,
   **zero entries, zero catch-up heads-ups**.
5. And even with the window open, the catch-up could not have taken the trade:
   it measures the move from the *fire*. AXISBANK at 12:10 was +4.3% from a 09:20
   entry with a ~1% stop — **4R**, past `SCAN5M_LEADER_MAX_R` (2R) — and its T1,
   the 09:20 pivot, was behind the price by lunchtime. The level that refused it
   was three hours old.

So the failure is not one missing exception. It is that **the catch-up asks the
bot to take the 09:20 trade at 12:10**. That trade is gone. What is still there
is a *new* trade: the sector qualifies, the leaders are still leading, and the
next candle's stop is 1R away.

## 7. The edge

> **When a sector becomes tradeable for the first time that day, its top two
> names are still trades — entered at that candle's close, stopped at that
> candle's EMA9, not at the levels of a fire from hours earlier.**

That is `LATE_LEADER_REBASE` (default on), applied where `PICK_CATCHUP` already
opens the window:

* the fire is older than `SCAN5M_LEADER_MAX_MINUTES` → the fire's entry, stop and
  targets are discarded, and `trade.plan_at` builds this candle's plan instead;
* the name must still be **running** (`momentum_verdict`, unchanged) and still
  **ahead of its sector** (the board's lead, unchanged);
* the sector must be the pick and the window must be open (`PICK_CATCHUP`), so it
  cannot fire on a candle that changed nothing;
* entry = the decision candle's close, stop = its EMA9 ± 0.02%, targets = the
  next pivots ahead *or* the measured fallback — the same builder, gates and
  ladder as a fresh entry, so `MIN_TARGET_R` and `TARGET_FALLBACK` apply;
* one alert per name per session, cap and `pick_cap_key` unchanged.

### What it would have done on this tape

Risk measured as the actual stop distance of the seven real trades (median
**1.05%**):

| sector | first tradeable | names | left after entry | R at a 1.05% stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BANK | 12:05–12:10 | AXISBANK (#2, lead +2.2), INDUSINDBK (#1, lead +6.7) | +1.73pp / +1.03pp | **+1.65R / +0.98R** |
| FIN | 11:50 | AXISBANK (#1), MFSL (#2) | +0.69pp / +1.13pp | **+0.66R / +1.08R** |
| REALTY | 12:55 | GODREJPROP (#1), LODHA (#2) | +1.36pp / +0.26pp | **+1.30R / +0.25R** |
| MEDIA | 12:05 | PVRINOX, PFOCUS | −1.36pp *against* a long | 0 — the bull tape's side rule refuses it |

**+5.9R over six added trades, against the +1.41R the day actually produced**,
and the two BANK trades alone are worth more than everything the bot did.

Honest bounds on that number:

* The exits are held to 15:10. A re-based trade trails EMA9 exactly like any
  other, so a name that stalled would have exited earlier and smaller — BANK's
  names made their gains in the last two hours (13:00–15:10), which is the
  friendliest case for a close-of-day exit. Call it **+3R to +5.9R**, not +5.9.
* One day. The mechanism (a sector that has been one-way for 10+ candles has
  leaders that keep going for another hour) is plausible and this tape is 3 for
  3 sectors, but 3 for 3 on one session is a hypothesis, not a track record.
* It adds up to 6 trades a day where the bot took 7. It cannot fire twice on the
  same sector, and it needs a sector to *become* qualified — on a day where the
  first pick is the right pick, it adds nothing.

### Why this is not the chase the 19 Nov rule refused

The old catch-up refused M&M at +2.0R because the room left to T1 was 0.04R of
the risk taken *from there*. That test is about a trade that has already paid.
This one is about a trade that has not started: the risk is 1R from the entry
candle, and the target ladder is rebuilt from the current price. The name has
been running all morning, but the *position* is new, and it is stoppable at 1R.

## 8. What changed in the code (this PR)

| change | knob | why |
|---|---|---|
| `trade.plan_at(side, frame, bar)` — the levels of a trade with no fire on the candle | — | `build_plan` needed a `StockSignal`; a re-based entry has none. Same builder, same gates. |
| `catchup_candidate(..., rebase=True)` hands the stale fire back with **no levels** | `LATE_LEADER_REBASE` | past the window, the fire is evidence, not levels |
| `late_plan` builds the re-based plan and the alert says so | `LATE_LEADER_REBASE=1` | the message must not pretend these are the fire's levels |
| `PICK_CATCHUP` actually opens the window | `PICK_CATCHUP=1` | the marker was one candle behind the pick; `_consider_late` is only reached inside the window now, so it says so instead of re-asking |
| `rebased_entries` counted, in the summary and the audit | — | "0 entries" is not a diagnosis; nor is an entry nobody counted |
| re-based hits never appear as heads-ups | — | there is nothing to report if there are no levels: it is an entry or nothing |

Both workflows forward `LATE_LEADER_REBASE`. `pytest -q`: **876 passed**.

## 9. What to look at tomorrow

1. **Did the re-base take a trade?** `rebased_entries` in the summary, and
   `INFRA`/`PSUBK`-style late switches in the audit. Expect it to fire on the
   first session where the pick moves after 11:00.
2. **The second book.** On this tape BANK was above the persistence floor from
   11:50 and FIN from 10:45 while IT held the pick on 85–99; only 8 of 15 sectors
   ever had a book at all, and the second-strongest gated sector was scanned for
   at most the last hour. The re-base makes a *late* pick tradeable; it does
   nothing for a sector that never becomes the pick. That is the next hole, and
   it needs the same kind of evidence before a rule.
3. **`risk_rs` is 0 on every trade.** Until it is fixed every R in the daily
   report is reconstructed, and the +1.41R above is not risk-sized.
4. **Costs are 28% of gross** (₹692 on ₹2,449). Six more trades of the re-base's
   size would have added ~₹600 of cost — the edge survives it, but this is the
   second day in a row that friction is a fifth of the result.
